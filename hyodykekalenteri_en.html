Commodity Calendar

A clear yearly overview for coffee, cocoa, soybeans and natural gas. The goal is not to pretend to have a crystal ball, but to offer a calm map: when to watch, when to consider a buying window, and when it may be wiser to keep your fingers away from the button like keeping a coffee cup away from the keyboard on a Monday morning.
